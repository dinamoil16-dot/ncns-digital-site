WEBSITE DISCLOSURE / 31 OCTOBER 2026

Privacy & contact

This website is under owner review. Our public email, Instagram and WhatsApp links have been configured. Automated project-form delivery has not yet been activated or independently verified. The operator's full legal identity, hosting provider and retention terms must be confirmed before public launch.

Local planning versus sending an enquiry

The Experience Lab builds a planning draft locally and does not automatically upload it. The project enquiry form keeps typed text in your browser until you deliberately select Send Project Request. If submission is enabled, the form transfers only the fields you provided to the contact provider to process and forward your enquiry; local copy remains a separate option. Do not include passwords, sensitive customer records or confidential documents.

Contact channels

Our confirmed Instagram profile is @ncns.digital. Opening it takes you to Instagram; messages you choose to send are handled by that platform. The website does not read your Instagram account or send messages on your behalf.

Our contact icons link to ncns.digital@gmail.com, Instagram, and WhatsApp. The email action opens your mail application; you must choose Send there. Opening Instagram or WhatsApp does not automatically submit your project brief. We cannot verify receipt of messages solely from a link click.

Direct project form and external processor

We are preparing Formspree as our form-processing provider. When the provider has been configured and you submit the form, your name, reply email, selected service, project description and any optional process/reply details will be transmitted to Formspree for processing and email notification to NCNS Digital. Formspree may retain form submissions and process technical request information under its own privacy policy and account settings. The form is currently inactive pending an owner-managed endpoint and an actual delivery check. Consent is requested before submission; direct email, Instagram and WhatsApp remain alternatives. Provider-specific storage, retention, international transfers and hosting details must be reviewed before launch.

Requests and necessary information

A business need, existing process, desired outcome and reply channel are enough for an initial discussion. If you contact NCNS directly, the information you choose to share is used to understand and respond to that enquiry. Ask about correction or deletion through the same verified channel used for the conversation. A production retention policy and responsible operator details remain a pre-launch requirement.

Technical data

No analytics SDK, advertising cookies, tracking pixels or embedded social widgets are included in this version. The contact configuration is a public file and contains no credentials. A hosting provider may process technical access logs; its identity, logging and retention settings must be confirmed before deployment. This page does not claim that hosting logs do not exist.

Updates

Any future analytics, account system or connected AI workflow needs an updated disclosure and appropriate access controls before it is enabled. Formspree submission will be activated only after endpoint setup and an owner-reviewed test. This notice is not a claim of completed legal review.
