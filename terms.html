SCOPE & EXPECTATIONS

Website terms

This website describes the services offered under the NCNS Digital brand. Its content is for general information and does not constitute a binding quote, contract, service-level agreement or guarantee of business results. Each paid engagement is agreed separately in writing.

Agree the work before it begins

Each engagement needs a written scope: deliverables, acceptance criteria, schedule, fees, payment terms, revisions, exclusions, access, ownership and handover. Optional maintenance or optimization has its own responsibilities and review cadence. Third-party costs and marketing budgets are separate and explicitly approved.

Local tools and indicative information

The Experience Lab creates a planning draft from your choices. It is not an AI agent, an automated quotation or a sent enquiry. Nothing on the website certifies feasibility, guarantees rankings, leads, revenue or profit, or claims a live integration that has not been scoped and tested.

Accounts, rights and materials

Clients should retain agreed access to their accounts, data and transferable deliverables. Ownership, licenses, confidentiality and any external assets are defined in the engagement agreement. Do not provide materials you are not authorized to share. The original NCNS emblem and this site's creative materials must not be presented as someone else's work.

Contact and platform links

Copying an Experience Lab draft does not contact NCNS. Form submission is not a service booking, acceptance of work or promise of a response time. The email icon opens a draft that you send yourself; Instagram and WhatsApp open the respective platforms. Technical delivery is not guaranteed for every message.
