# Approved visual direction — NCNS Digital

Approved by owner: **Cinematic Luxury × Premium Minimalism (concepts 1 + 3)**, 30 October 2026.

## Exact brand constraints
- Original metallic NCNS DIGITAL emblem with circular silver/ice-blue halo. Repository asset: \`assets/ncns-original-logo.webp\`, recovered unchanged from the owner's previously approved NCNS site. **Do not redraw or synthesize new letters.**
- Dark black/navy composition, silver metallic type, subtle ice-blue rim lights. **No neon/acid green**, purple gradients, generic template hero or bright multi-colour decoration.
- Visually: premium international studio, compelling cinematographic lighting, carefully restrained type, spacious layout.

## Scene, layout and interactions
- First screen: huge metallic emblem and animated ring **on the right**; statement and CTAs **on the left**; atmospheric dark jagged mountains and a reflective waterline in the lower half.
- Navbar: small wordmark, Services, Work, Process, About/Contact, one clear proposal CTA.
- Services below first screen: minimalist 3-card section for websites, branding, AI & automation; high-quality restrained 3D-like artwork or lightweight handcrafted illustration.
- Later sections: Selected Work (only factual portfolio demos, clearly marked "Concept" if fictional), four-step process, clear contact.
- Motion: gently breathing ring light, shimmering glints, slow cloud/mist/water highlights, shallow pointer parallax, progressive reveal on scroll, physically calm card hover. Respect reduced motion and mobile performance.

## Fidelity rules
- The owner-approved collage was a speculative *image reference*, not actual site code. **Never use the full composition as a flat hero/background screenshot.**
- Future reference files can be reviewed with the owner, but the shipped site must be independent HTML/CSS/JS components with real interactions.
- No real client testimonials, counts, trust badges, brand partners or SEO metrics without proof.
- Do not invent a real booking or form backend.
- Strong first milestone: polished hero + header + 3 services, screenshots at 1440px and 390px, functional visual motion and interactions. Stop for owner approval before expanding to fictional portfolio.

## Visual QA acceptance
1. Original logo is legible and undistorted at mobile and desktop widths.
2. No overflowing navigation, clipped CTA, horizontal scroll or obstructed copy.
3. Hero focal balance left-copy / right-logo remains strong on desktop; mobile stacks logically.
4. Motion is subtle and functional, disabled or reduced for users requesting less motion.
5. Website actually works, not a static screenshot.
