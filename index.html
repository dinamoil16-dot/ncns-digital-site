BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful answers.

Clear expectations make a better starting point.

Can we start with one small problem?+

Yes. A focused discovery can identify the smallest useful deliverable: an enquiry path, a planning tool, a brand system or a workflow. A full digital transformation is not a prerequisite.

How are cost and timing agreed?+

After discussing the problem, existing systems and acceptance criteria, we define deliverables, exclusions, revisions and a realistic schedule. Third-party services and budgets are stated separately; this site does not issue a binding quote.

Do we need an ongoing service?+

No. A project can finish with documentation and handover. Optional Operate or Grow work has a separate agreed scope, responsibilities and review cadence. Continued value should be visible, not dependent on lock-in.

Are AI and integrations already connected here?+

No. The Experience Lab assembles a draft locally. Real AI, CRM, messaging or payment integrations require a separate scope, authorized access, evaluation and appropriate data handling.

What should we share first?+

Your business need, existing website or process, desired outcome and a reply channel are enough to begin. Do not send passwords, sensitive customer records or confidential documents through the planning tool.
