# NCNS Digital — approved full service catalog / Утверждённый перечень услуг

**Approved by owner: 31 October 2026. Stable source of truth for brand positioning, copy, service architecture and future portfolio.**

NCNS Digital is an **international AI & Digital Agency** serving companies **in different industries worldwide** (not limited to automotive, detailing, one country, or one niche). Positioning: **Digital solutions that work** — attract customers, improve conversion, simplify operations, automate repetitive work and grow intelligently.

This file records **12 approved service families**. It is our **offerings and development roadmap**, **NOT** a claim that every named complex integration or autonomous agent is already built, live, tested or universally supported. Scope, feasibility, permissions, data security, costs and delivery terms must be clarified for each client. Never fabricate case studies, past clients, metrics, endorsements or guaranteed outcomes.

Logo and visual identity development; color/typography systems; brand books; brand guidelines; social media profile packaging; presentations and pitch decks; proposal design; advertising assets; product and packaging concepts.

Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn packaging; social bio and highlights; visual grids; posts and content plans; ongoing editorial content; Google Business Profile optimization; digital identity consistency across channels.

### 8. SEO & Website Optimization / Поисковая оптимизация
Technical and on-page SEO; site architecture; site speed and Core Web Vitals improvements; mobile UX; local SEO; search-oriented content structure; user-path and conversion optimization; accessibility and usability improvements.

### 11. Integrations & Custom Solutions / Интеграции и индивидуальная разработка
CRM, websites, messaging, email, forms, spreadsheets, payment providers and other APIs when available and authorized; bespoke workflow tools; internal dashboards; service connectors; custom systems where off-the-shelf solutions do not meet requirements.

### 12. Maintenance & Growth / Поддержка и развитие

## Approved website navigation — 5 clear service pillars

Display 5 user-friendly groups on the public website rather than overwhelming visitors with a 12-item top-level menu. All 12 families MUST be representable under these groups:

1. **Web & Digital Products:** websites, web apps, e-commerce, configurators, calculators.
2. **AI & Automation:** AI assistants, agents, bots, CRM and workflow automation.
3. **Branding & Creative:** visual identity, content, AI video, motion and brand assets.
4. **Marketing & Growth:** social presence, SEO, lead generation, conversion and analytics.
5. **Custom Business Solutions:** custom systems, integrations, business discovery, maintenance and ongoing growth.

This is an information architecture, not permission to silently discard any of the 12 approved service families from NCNS's full catalog.

## Portfolio strategy — approved goal, not yet delivered

**Target: 5–6 high-quality, distinctly designed interactive DEMO projects from different global industries.** A visitor opens each demo and tests real interactive functionality, rather than only viewing a static mockup. Each demo must prove a specific mix of design, web development, interaction, sales workflow and/or AI/automation, with honest boundaries between real functionality and simulation.

- Each project must have distinctive premium visual direction and a working interaction (quote calculator, custom configurator, intake wizard, branded commerce UI, automation simulator, etc.).
- Clearly label fictional companies and concept prices as **Concept / Interactive Demo**. Do not represent demo websites as paid client work, working live backends or genuine customer results.
- **Milestone C1** (Issue #6) initially designs **three** visual concepts for review, desktop and mobile. Three is **phase one**, not the total portfolio limit. Plan the broader **5–6 demos** without building them before visual owner approval.
- Choose industries for diversity, not a narrow focus on the USA, detailing or automotive. The owner approves each creative direction before implementation.
- Keep the already approved agency website and emblem intact; do not deploy or merge unapproved portfolio work.
