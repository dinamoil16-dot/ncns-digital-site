# NCNS Digital — Instagram inbound and client-acquisition strategy
**Owner-approved direction 2026-10-31.** A global B2B agency with 12 service families, built around Build → Operate → Grow. Instagram is **a visual proof and organic inbound channel**, supplementing email and LinkedIn outreach, not replacing them. The objective is qualified business enquiries and recurring clients, not artificial follower counts.

## Agency account and conversion
- Display name: NCNS Digital | AI & Growth Systems.
- Suggested English bio (adjust to Instagram bio limits): Interactive websites • AI agents • Automation / Digital systems that turn attention into business / Explore the demos ↓
- Use the original NCNS emblem and a coherent premium black/silver/ice-blue identity. English-first, global audiences; do not brand the agency around a temporary location.
- One link to a **working** Explore Solutions page as soon as it is publicly available. Until then link only to an actually accessible resource.
- Highlights: Work / Services / AI / Growth / Process / Contact.
- Three pinned posts: (1) business problems NCNS solves, (2) best genuine working interactive demo, (3) process and invitation to a project review.
- All concept projects must be visibly labeled Concept / Interactive Demo, not fake client successes.

## Content pillars (planning proportions)
1. **35% Cinematic interactive showcases**: 6–20s authentic demonstrations of selectable 3D yacht sections, interactive jacket/architectural cutaway, product customizers, animated data workflows. A compelling gesture in the first 1–2s, then a tangible buyer result. Label nonfunctional motion concepts honestly.
2. **25% Business pain → NCNS system**: short lead/CRM problems and live demonstrations of solutions with clearly fictional sample data; illustrate a process improvement mechanism, never invented client ROI.
3. **20% Useful B2B education**: why enquiries are lost, what to automate first, lead volume versus profit, qualified leads, conversion, handoff from AI to humans, baseline metrics.
4. **10% Build in public and craft**: actual development recordings, 3D process, before/after design, testing and performance decisions; no confidential code or customer data.
5. **10% Trust and offers**: scope, Build → Operate → Grow, mini-audit format, what the client receives, honest project discovery and limitations.

## Suggested sustainable weekly publishing
- **3 original Reels per week**: two interactive product clips and one clear business-problem/solution clip.
- **2 carousels per week**: one educational, one service/solution breakdown.
- **Stories on 3–5 days a week**: progress, honest behind-the-scenes, helpful answers, polls and new demos.
- One more substantial concept case recap per month. Adapt the pace to ability to produce truly original quality content.
- Face/voice are optional: good silent screen recordings with large readable English captions work for this brand; optional approved voiceover later.
- Repurpose relevant material into LinkedIn with new B2B-specific editorial framing.

## First four weeks
Week 1 — profile, identity and three pin concepts; agency value explanation; show Growth OS action workflow rather than just pretty charts; practical carousel about lost leads.
Week 2 — actual Growth OS video and manual-to-automated funnel explanation; one short motion-design study; give a usable project brief CTA.
Week 3 — cinematic 3D interaction concept (yacht, industrial, product or home) and how the interaction informs purchase; 2 educational carousels on quotes/CRM; one process clip.
Week 4 — strongest functioning demo recap; Build → Operate → Grow explainer; audit-style Q&A post; inspect results and change plan.
Days 31–90 — repeat winning themes, produce clips for each new functional demo and test original hooks while keeping a business-focused CTA. Instagram growth and contract outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

## Practical original Reel scripts
R1 — "This is what happens AFTER a customer clicks Submit." Clip shows original sample ad → qualified intake → operator CRM → next action. End: "We build the system behind the page."
R2 — "This catalog opens products. Not just thumbnails." A garment unzip, exploded product, or interactive house cutaway, followed by feature/configuration output. Until live, overlay "Concept animation."
R3 — "Your business doesn't need another dashboard. It needs the NEXT ACTION." Sample overdue follow-up visibly moves through a real Growth OS UI. End: "Explore our interactive demo."

## Inbound funnel
Original visual Reel/carousel → business owner watches → NCNS profile → actual interactive demo or service explanation → human-reviewed DM or working project enquiry → light qualification → audit and proposal → scoped Build + optional Operate/Grow engagement.

Suggested non-spammy CTAs: "Explore the interactive demo"; "DM DEMO for a relevant sample"; "Request a project review". Respond manually or through approved platform-compliant tools; avoid unsolicited bulk DMs.

Light qualification: their business/industry, costly process, current tool/workflow, desired outcome, timing, approximate scope, primary decision maker. Do not request unnecessary sensitive information. Only promise a personalized mini-audit if there is delivery capacity.

## Measurement / weekly review
Track nonfollower reach, average watch time/completion (when provided), shares/sends/saves, profile visits, site clicks, qualified DMs, booked calls, proposals, paid contracts and retained client work. Evaluate where people drop off and prioritize qualified conversions over follower growth. Track campaign origins via tagged links where practical and privacy-respecting.

Experiments: 10s wow vs 20s interaction/business explanation; video hooks; try demo vs request audit CTA; organic Reel format; multilingual caption if actual international demand justifies it. Trial Reels may test ideas with nonfollowers if the business account has access. Ads only after proven organic concept and separate explicit budget approval.

## Safety and truth
No purchased followers, engagement rings, fake case studies, false ROI, intrusive tracking, mass-follow bots or unauthorized DMs. Display sample results as fictional. A real model/API/service is not present unless actually integrated and tested. Publishing, messaging and ads need explicit owner authorization and proper connected access. This doc does not schedule or publish anything.
